It's for anyone who has a list of friends' birthdays and keeps forgetting them. You add birthdays once, and a server sends the reminder every morning, even if you haven't opened the app. It works in a phone's web browser and can be added to the home screen so it looks and behaves like a regular app.

## What you can do with it

- See whose birthday is next right at the top, and the rest of the year laid out month by month with how many days are left.
- Catch anyone you nearly missed: birthdays from the past week stay at the top instead of dropping to the far end of the year.
- See how old each person is turning, if you know the year they were born. The year is optional, because plenty of lists don't have it.
- Add and edit birthdays by hand.
- Import a whole list at once from a spreadsheet (CSV) or data file (JSON). It shows you exactly what it read and what it couldn't before anything is saved, and importing the same file twice won't make duplicates.
- Turn on morning reminders on your phone, and send yourself a test notification to check they arrive.
- Open it without a signal and still see the last list you looked at.
- Keep the list behind a passcode, typed once on each phone or laptop you use.
